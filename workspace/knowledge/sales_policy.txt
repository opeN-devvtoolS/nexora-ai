SALES & DISCOUNT POLICY (FY2026)
================================

1. DISCOUNT LIMITS
Sales reps may approve discounts up to 15% on their own. Discounts from 16% to 25% need written approval from the regional manager. Discounts above 25% need approval from the VP of Sales and are only allowed for Corporate customers ordering at least 20 units.

2. COMMISSION
Reps earn 4% commission on net revenue for Electronics and Furniture, and 6% for Software. Office Supplies earn 2%. Commission is reduced by half on any order with a discount above 20%. Commission is paid quarterly, 30 days after quarter end.

3. RETURNS
Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. Software licenses can only be returned within 14 days and only if the key has not been activated. If an order is returned, the commission on it is taken back in the next payout.

4. DELIVERY TARGETS
Standard delivery target is 5 days. Orders delivered in more than 7 days must be reported to the logistics team. The West region has a longer target of 7 days because of warehouse distance.

5. PAYMENT TERMS
Credit Card and PayPal payments are charged at order time. Bank Transfer orders must be paid within 14 days. Cash on Delivery is only available for orders under 500 in total value.
