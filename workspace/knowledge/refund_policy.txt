REFUND POLICY
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

LATE REFUNDS
Requests made between 31 and 60 days after purchase receive store credit only, not money back. After 60 days no refund is possible.

DIGITAL PRODUCTS
Digital products such as e-books and software licenses are refundable within 7 days, as long as they have not been downloaded more than once.

HOW TO REQUEST
Email support@example.com with your order number. Refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days to the original payment method.
